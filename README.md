# OmniMart Business & Marketing Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Title & Short Description

**OmniMart Business & Marketing Analytics Dashboard | Power BI**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze **sales, store performance, product performance, profitability, marketing effectiveness, and month-over-month business trends** across OmniMart's retail and e-commerce operations.

The dashboard transforms historical business data into actionable insights through KPI tracking, performance comparisons, trend analysis, and interactive filtering.

---

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this project is to provide a centralized business intelligence dashboard that helps decision-makers:

* Monitor overall revenue, units sold, gross profit, and gross margin.
* Track month-over-month business performance.
* Identify top and bottom-performing stores and products.
* Evaluate marketing spend and return on advertising spend (ROAS).
* Compare retail store and e-commerce performance.
* Identify areas of strong and weak business performance.
* Support data-driven decisions around sales, products, stores, and marketing investments.

---

## 3. Tech Stack

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development & data visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Measures, KPIs, time-based calculations and business metrics
* **Microsoft Excel** – Data source / historical business data
* **Data Modeling** – Relationships between fact and dimension tables
* **Data Visualization** – KPI cards, trend charts, bar charts, tables and interactive slicers

---

## 4. Data Source

**Source:** Historical company/business data provided for the OmniMart case study.

The dataset represents historical performance across:

* **20 retail stores**
* **E-commerce operations**
* Products
* Sales transactions
* Marketing campaigns
* Marketing spend
* Store performance
* Product performance

The data was transformed and modeled in Power BI to create a reporting layer suitable for business analysis.

> **Note:** The project uses historical/synthetic case-study data for portfolio and analytical demonstration purposes and does not represent confidential company data.

## 6. Business Impact & Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify **where revenue and profitability are being generated and where improvement opportunities exist**.

### Key business insights include:

* Identifying the **highest and lowest-performing stores** helps management prioritize store-level actions and resource allocation.
* Product-level revenue and gross-profit rankings highlight **high-value products** as well as products requiring further investigation.
* Month-over-month trends provide visibility into **business growth, fluctuations, and changes in profitability**.
* Gross margin analysis helps distinguish between **high-revenue performance and genuinely profitable performance**.
* Marketing spend and ROAS analysis enables businesses to evaluate whether **marketing investment is translating into measurable returns**.
* Combining sales, profitability, store, product, and marketing metrics provides a **single view of overall business performance**.

### Overall Business Value

The dashboard converts raw historical business data into a **decision-support tool**, allowing stakeholders to quickly identify performance gaps, monitor KPIs, evaluate marketing efficiency, and focus on areas with the greatest potential for business improvement.
